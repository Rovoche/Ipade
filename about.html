About

A studio built around one question.

Founder

Kemi Adewale

Founder & Creative Director

Kemi founded IPADÉ after nine years planning events for hotels and hospitality groups across Lagos. She started the studio around one observation: most event planning treats every gathering the same way, regardless of why it's happening. A wedding, a company dinner and a fortieth birthday need to be built differently, because the people in the room want different things from the evening.

Her approach starts with that question before it starts with a mood board. That's the reason IPADÉ takes on a limited number of events each season rather than as many as it can book.

Philosophy

"A gathering has a purpose before it has a guest list. Once we understand what that purpose is, most of the design decisions make themselves."

How We Work

The same four steps, whether it's eight guests or eight hundred.

Listen

We start with the people, not a mood board: who's gathering, why now, and what the day is meant to do for them.

Imagine

We develop the creative direction: the room, the run of show and the guest experience, before a single vendor is booked.

Produce

We manage the vendors, the timeline, and the hundred small decisions that happen between the plan and the day.

Gather

On the day, our job is to be the only people working. Everyone else gets to be present.
