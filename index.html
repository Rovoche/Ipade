The Studio

IPADÉ plans and produces gatherings, from an eight-seat dinner to a five-hundred-guest wedding.

We work from a simple premise: an event succeeds when everything in the room (the seating, the lighting, the pacing of the evening) serves the reason people came together. That takes as much planning on the run of show as it does on the flowers. We plan for the guest list you actually have, not an imagined bigger version of it.

The Approach

Beautiful events take more than decoration.

Listen

We start with the people, not a mood board: who's gathering, why now, and what the day is meant to do for them.

Imagine

We develop the creative direction: the room, the run of show and the guest experience, before a single vendor is booked.

Produce

We manage the vendors, the timeline, and the hundred small decisions that happen between the plan and the day.

Gather

On the day, our job is to be the only people working. Everyone else gets to be present.

Tradition

Built with the families who carry it

Traditional ceremonies aren't a smaller version of what we do. They're some of the most demanding events we plan. Engagement lists, aso-ebi coordination, family protocol and the sequence of the ceremony all have to be right, and right for that specific family. We plan traditional ceremonies alongside contemporary elements when a family wants both, and exactly as tradition asks when they don't.

Corporate

Events that still have to work as business

Conferences, product launches, staff dinners and investor events, planned around an agenda and not just a party. We coordinate speakers, run-of-show, AV and catering as one schedule, so the event still does its job: get a message across, on time, to the people who came for it.
