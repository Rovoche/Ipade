Experiences

The full range of what we plan.

Five categories, one way of working: understand why people are gathering, then design everything in the room around that. Some events need a stage and three hundred chairs. Others need a single table set exactly right.

Weddings

Contemporary weddings, planned from the first venue walk-through to the last song of the night. We handle the vendors, the timeline and the moments in between, the parts that make the day feel like yours rather than a template.

Traditional Celebrations

Engagements, introductions and traditional weddings. We coordinate directly with families on protocol, aso-ebi and the sequence of the ceremony, so tradition is handled correctly and not treated as decoration.

Private Celebrations

Birthdays, anniversaries and milestone dinners for guest lists you could actually name. Smaller events get the same planning discipline as large ones. Nothing here is treated as an afterthought.

Corporate Events

Conferences, launches, staff dinners and investor events. We build a single run-of-show that covers speakers, AV, catering and logistics, so the event still does its job as business.

Event Styling & Production

Venue transformations, florals, tablescapes and catering coordination for hosts who already have a planner in place and need the creative and production side handled well.

Not sure which category fits your event?

Tell us what you're planning and we'll tell you how we'd approach it.
